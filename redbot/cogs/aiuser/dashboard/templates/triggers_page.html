Trigger words & information

When a message contains a trigger phrase, the bot replies using its saved information. The AI can vary the wording while keeping the facts and links intact.

Matches ignore letter case and use whole words or phrases: “site” matches “our site?” but not “opposite”. Put alternative phrases on separate lines. Replies follow the existing enabled channels, member permissions, consent and reply-cost settings.
